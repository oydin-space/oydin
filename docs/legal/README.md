# Oydin legal texts and release conditions

Updated: 2026-11-18. Working legal copy, not a licensed Uzbek legal opinion. The requested Markdown format is intentional.

## Deliverables and integration contract

- `web/src/content/legal/{ru,uz,en}.ts`: nine texts, three documents per language. `types.ts` fixes identical section keys for each language; `index.ts` exports bundles. Uzbek Latin prevails, subject to mandatory law. Native-speaker and lawyer approval remains required.
- `/privacy` and `/terms`: App Router pages, server renderer, dated content, section anchors and language switch. Reuse the existing `lang` cookie, `parseLocale` and `LanguageSwitcher`; no competing locale storage. The visible main/fieldset sets its own `lang` because the existing root is Russian.
- `ConsentNotice({ locale, disabled? })`: render inside a sign-up/onboarding form. `consent_service` is required agreement acceptance, **not** a bundled privacy consent. `consent_ai`, `consent_partners`, `consent_benchmarks`, `consent_emails` are independent native checkboxes, all initially unchecked, never required. Checked value is `yes`; unchecked fields are absent. Hidden `legal_version` and `legal_locale` identify the displayed text.
- The parent screen must persist each affirmative/negative choice, authenticated subject, version, locale, timestamp and evidence of the exact text; do not infer consent from an omitted field on an update. Backend must enforce AI and optional-use gates before processing. This component neither persists nor grants consent by itself. A withdrawal UI must be as easy as granting, with electronic requests also accepted. Sign-in/onboarding files and backend are outside this assignment and have not been wired here.
- Email unsubscribe, recipient-specific referrals, owner-only business deletion, account deletion, export, link revocation and provider erasure need real flows. The texts do not assert those flows were tested. All three texts visibly warn about incomplete operator details and unconfirmed hosting/transfer safeguards. Do not treat publication of this copy as production legal clearance.

## Current Uzbekistan law: primary sources checked

Checked online 2026-11-18. LexUZ's Russian consolidated text labels several 2026 amendments an **unofficial translation**. A licensed Uzbek lawyer must apply the official Uzbek text and any later implementing rules, not rely solely on this English summary.

1. [ZRU-547 of 2 July 2019, consolidated Russian text](https://www.lex.uz/acts/4396428) and [official Uzbek text](https://lex.uz/acts/4396419). Articles 10, 17–19 require purpose-limited storage/processing and destruction when applicable. Article 18 includes consent, contract, legal duty and lawful interests conditional on rights. Do not invent a GDPR-only legal basis.
2. Article 21: ordinary consent may be in any form that proves receipt; special-category processing and consent for minors have written/electronic-document requirements. Withdrawal may use the original form or written/electronic form. Articles 22–23 require information and written notices, including third-party disclosure; article 23 states a three-day third-party-transfer notice requirement. A consent checkbox is not automatically every required notice or electronic document.
3. Articles 11, 22, 24, 30–31: correction/completion (article 11: no later than three days on request; inaccurate data corrected without delay), information, access details, withdrawal, suspension of incomplete/outdated/inaccurate/unlawful/unnecessary processing, electronic requests and complaints to authority/court. Article 22 refusal notice: written within ten days. Article 24 automated consequential decisions have additional requirements and a ten-day objection response. No universal GDPR-style one-month response or statutory portability promise is asserted.
4. Article 15: adequate-protection country route and specified alternatives, including consent for transfer to non-adequate states, subject to prohibitions/restrictions. **This does not replace article 27¹ storage conditions.**
5. [ZRU-1125 of 26 March 2026](https://lex.uz/acts/8104580), reflected in current articles 20 and 27¹: mandatory Uzbekistan storage covers biometric data, genetic data and data of users of telecommunications operators operating in Uzbekistan. Other personal data may be stored/processed abroad under an adequate-country route, compliant standard contractual clauses/binding corporate rules, or recognised international standards. The older blanket-localisation description is not a safe statement of current law. Article 20 now links registration to databases subject to mandatory domestic storage under article 27¹(2); it also retains exemptions and change-notification rules. Distinguish database registration from an assumed universal operator licence.
6. [Cabinet Resolution 415 of 29 July 2026, effective 3 August](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8369688), [Russian metadata](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8371371): official list of 49 adequate-protection jurisdictions. **United States coverage is limited to companies operating within the EU–US Data Privacy Framework**, not every US processor. Paragraph 4 includes conditions for non-listed-country transfers and, for leaks during cross-border transfers, authority notice within 24 hours and detailed information within 72 hours. Paragraph 7 calls for implementing SCC/BCR requirements. Confirm the latest implementing acts and each recipient's status; a US brand name is not enough.
7. Other 2026 changes visible in the consolidated act: [ZRU-1158 of 13 July 2026](https://lex.uz/acts/8325838), special legal regime (article 3), and [ZRU-1174 of 11 September 2026](https://lex.uz/acts/8487669), child-care processing exception (article 25). Do not assume ZRU-1125 is the last amendment.
8. [Cabinet Resolution 71 of 8 February 2020](https://lex.uz/acts/4732190), database register procedures, and [model processing procedure, registered 3478](https://lex.uz/acts/6663971) / [responsible-function procedure, registered 3477](https://lex.uz/acts/6668412), linked by the consolidated law. Their current interaction with 2026 amendments requires counsel review; they were identified, not exhaustively interpreted here.

## Licensed Uzbek lawyer: decisions required before real-data launch

- Verify the operator/database owner, address, monitored contact, legal status and jurisdiction. Approve all nine texts with a native Uzbek legal-language review and the Uzbek-precedence clause.
- Establish actual server, backups, OpenRouter routing/subprocessors, Gemini, Anthropic, Langfuse, Google, Telegram, integration and email/TTS/OCR processor countries, contracts, access and retention. Determine the valid article 27¹ route for **each** foreign store and onward transfer, and the applicable article 15 basis. Check recipient DPF certification if relying on US adequacy. Consent alone is not a localisation solution.
- Assess whether ordinary audio, receipt faces or other uploads are biometric under articles 26/27¹. The current article 27¹ wording is not safely reduced to only voiceprints used for identification. If mandatory domestic storage applies, resolve hosting and database registration before collection. Assess Telegram identifiers versus the telecom-user category; do not assume they automatically qualify or do not qualify.
- Confirm current registry procedure, exemptions and change notices, responsible privacy person/function, security levels, model internal procedures and special-territory rules if relevant. Do not claim a registration certificate without one.
- Approve evidence of consent, written/electronic-document validity, third-party notices within three days, fresh purpose/recipient consent, withdrawal behavior and separate optional choices. Check automated-decision limits. No inference that account agreement alone authorises AI transfers or marketing.
- Adopt exact retention and deletion schedules for records, logs, original uploads, local offline copies, backups, provider copies and consent evidence; verify identity checks, exports, business-owner authority, shared-record rights and breach response (including Resolution 415's 24/72-hour duties where applicable). No promises of instant universal erasure.
- Validate anonymous lead and benchmark designs, small-cohort re-identification risks, bank/MFO contracts, future contact capture and whether any credit intermediation or financial regulation applies. Anonymous leads must not silently contain identifying contact details.
- Confirm marketing/email rules, cookies and offline notices, children exclusion and handling of accidental child/special-category data. No sensitive-data consent is bundled here.
- Confirm free/request-access plan statements, future paid contracting/refunds, Uzbek consumer-law applicability and mandatory remedies. Approve liability language without an unlawful waiver. Confirm governing law and courts; tax disclaimer does not immunise negligence.
- Reconcile stale `docs/product.md` tax-regime/calculator claims with the explicit assignment and `docs/research/tax-fit.md` disclaimer: legal texts say **no tax-due calculation, filing or payment**, only user-set cash reserves and confirmed-date reminders. Any contrary shipped calculator/copy needs product-owner action outside this assignment.
