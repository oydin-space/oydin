# Copy the useful habit, not the finance department

Research date: 2026-11-18. Delivery: standard; omissions: none. Research only; no product changes.

**Recommendation:** borrow emerging-market apps’ quick entry and person-by-person debt clarity, then add a trustworthy cash decision. Do not build a smaller ERP. Finmir already advertises UZS, Telegram entry and cash-gap forecasts: these are category requirements, not a unique Oydin claim.

## Evidence and scope

Read first: [product](../product.md), [owner decisions](../decisions.md), [custdev](custdev.md), [market](market.md). Custdev is desk research with **zero own interviews**; its numbered pains below are research signals/hypotheses, not measured customer prevalence. Existing market research contains historical prices and weakly supported uniqueness claims; neither is repeated as current evidence here.

**Verified here** means the feature is explicitly described on a primary public page opened during this research, not independently exercised in a paid account. Vendor outcome claims (faster collection, safety, forecast accuracy, theft prevention) are not verified outcomes. App-store reviews are individual reports, not a representative defect rate. **Proposal** marks Oydin design decisions, not competitor facts or shipped functionality. Each product has 3–6 concrete mechanics below; linked sources apply to all items in that row.

The owner’s frozen phase contract takes precedence over older docs: optional user-set tax reserve/reminders, no tax calculators; text Telegram digest, no streaks; only own-history seasonality; unverified integrations remain coming soon. These are assignment constraints, not findings about competitors.

## 3. Ranked top-12 ADOPT/ADAPT backlog

**Proposal, not shipped scope or validated demand.** Ranking follows stronger custdev signals before attractive automation. It refines existing agreed surfaces rather than creating twelve new modules. Specs include acceptance behaviour for a later implementation owner; this research assignment writes no tests/code.

### 1. ADAPT — Close today without hiding a discrepancy (M)

From Pulse, show each cash/card/bank account’s expected closing amount, ask for the counted/confirmed amount, and display the unexplained difference in UZS with author/time. The owner can attach an explanation or leave it visibly unresolved; never manufacture an income/expense to force a match. Opening balances and subsequent corrections remain traceable. Acceptance: changing one counted balance changes its difference, not historical profit. Custdev pains 2/6; bank history is an input pattern, not proof competitors already provide this close ([Uzum](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=uz.kapitalbank.mobile24)).

### 2. ADAPT — Make “money”, “earned”, and “taken home” different (L)

Pulse and the period result distinguish confirmed cash, committed/reserved cash, earned result and owner draws/contributions. An advance is cash but not automatically earned; transfers between own accounts are not spending. If costs or fulfilment are unknown, explain the missing inputs instead of calculating a precise “safe to take” amount. Acceptance: owner contribution increases cash, not sales/profit; a customer advance cannot alone produce earned profit. Custdev pains 1/5; adapt [Finmir’s advance distinction](https://www.finmir.uz/en), not its chart of accounts.

### 3. ADAPT — First useful pulse, not first completed settings screen (M)

Offer demo or real business, choose segment/language, enter a starting amount and one upcoming payment, then show a provisional pulse with missing-data labels. File and three voice notes are optional accelerators; manual numbers remain available without bank credentials. Persist progress and explain login versus registration plainly. Measure a 60-second target in a pilot; do not claim it achieved. Acceptance: an owner reaches a real, explicitly incomplete result without configuring integrations. Custdev pains 7/8; [OkCredit setup](https://okcredit.in/en/) and [BILLZ registration review](https://apps.apple.com/uz/app/billz-2-0-mobile/id6444280664).

### 4. ADAPT — One entry bar, explicit confirmation (M)

Provide income, expense, own transfer, owner contribution/draw and debt/promise choices in ordinary words. Text and optional voice/Telegram produce the same editable draft: amount, account/currency, date and business; ambiguous fields ask one targeted question. Confirmation shows a durable saved receipt and edit path. Acceptance: a retry cannot create a second operation, and a seller sees only their own entries. Custdev pains 2/5/8; [OkCredit two-action ledger](https://okcredit.in/en/) and [Finmir shorthand](https://www.finmir.uz/en).

### 5. ADAPT — Import safely, then remember confirmed mappings (L)

Operations import previews CSV/XLSX rows, guessed columns/categories, currencies and possible duplicates before a user confirms the batch. Let owners fix a mapping once and reuse it; reconcile imported totals to the file and disclose rejected rows. Use only verified file formats/integrations from the frozen contract; bank-specific presets stay coming soon. Acceptance: repeat import does not double cash, and transfers/fees remain separate. Custdev pains 2/8; [Finmir import rules](https://www.finmir.uz/en), [Planfact import](https://planfact.io/features/platezhnyi-kalendar).

### 6. ADOPT — Rent entered once, paid only when confirmed (M)

Calendar stores amount, due date, recurrence and optional reserve per obligation. Future occurrences are planned money, not posted payments; link a confirmed operation to settle one occurrence without deducting it again in the forecast. Tax is an optional user-named reserve/deadline, not a calculated tax liability. Acceptance: paying rent removes that planned outflow once and leaves next month’s obligation intact. Custdev pain 9; [Adesk recurring payments](https://adesk.ru/calendar/).

### 7. ADAPT — A forecast that states its assumptions (L)

Lead with “On [date], [amount] may be missing for [payment]”, then a short daily list and accessible balance chart. Separate confirmed balances/commitments from expected receipts; show source dates, stale FX, excluded unknowns and a conservative no-unconfirmed-receipts view. Never call a promise cash or extrapolate universal seasonality. Acceptance: settling a planned payment does not double-count it; a missing rate prevents a misleading combined UZS total. Custdev pains 3/9 remain partly hypotheses; [Finmir](https://www.finmir.uz/en), [Planfact](https://planfact.io/features/platezhnyi-kalendar), [Float](https://www.floatapp.com/product).

### 8. ADOPT — “Who owes me?” with a believable date (M)

A compact customer card lists amount due, partial receipts, promised date and owner-entered confidence; “date unknown” is valid. Show the running balance and keep a clear link between a promise and later payment, without exposing a whole customer database to sellers. Acceptance: a partial receipt reduces debt and raises cash exactly once; future promises remain outside cash today. Custdev pain 9; [OkCredit customer balance](https://okcredit.in/en/) and [Float expected payment dates](https://www.floatapp.com/product).

### 9. ADAPT — “Can I afford this?” without changing the books (M)

From Pulse, enter a purchase amount/date or delay one expected receipt; compare baseline and scenario’s lowest balance, shortfall date and affected obligations. Restore/discard must be obvious; a favourable answer is qualified by missing data and does not create a payment or credit referral. Acceptance: simulation leaves all operations and commitments unchanged; a delayed customer receipt visibly shifts the result. Custdev pains 3/4; [Float toggle scenarios](https://www.floatapp.com/product) and [Finmir scenario lab](https://www.finmir.uz/en).

### 10. ADAPT — A polite reminder the owner actually approves (M)

From a debt card, generate a RU/UZ-latin/EN draft containing customer, balance, agreed date and business name, with editable text and a native share/copy path. Preview recipient and amount; never bulk-send or upload contacts by default. Separate “shared” from “delivered/read/paid” unless supported evidence exists. Acceptance: cancelling the share sends nothing and does not mark the debt paid. Custdev pains 9/10; [Vyapar templates and preview](https://vyaparapp.in/guides/how-to-send-payment-reminders-in-vyapar), [Khatabook language reminders](https://khatabook.com/en/).

### 11. ADAPT — Quick entry survives a bad connection (L)

PWA queues local drafts with visible “on this device, not synced” status, chosen business/account and a stable submission identity. Reconnection confirms once; conflicts require review rather than overwriting another user’s correction. Avoid storing more sensitive data offline than needed and never present unsynced drafts as confirmed server balances. Acceptance: offline → reconnect → retry yields one confirmed entry; changing business does not reassign the queued draft. Custdev pains 2/8; [OkCredit offline pattern](https://okcredit.in/en/), with reliability details proposed for Oydin.

### 12. ADAPT — One concise Telegram digest, with a scoped handoff (M)

Owner opts into cadence/quiet time and sees a text summary of confirmed balance, next material obligation, shortfall if any and one next action; unknowns remain visible. A period result can be exported/shared through the already agreed read-only report/PDF surface, with business, period, generation time and limited access; a recipient never inherits team permissions. Acceptance: opt-out stops notifications; revoked/expired links cannot reveal current reports. Custdev pains 8/10; [Adesk weekly bot indicators](https://adesk.ru/calendar/), [OkCredit PDF statements](https://okcredit.in/en/) and [Float export](https://www.floatapp.com/product). Desired cadence and willingness to share remain hypotheses.

## 4. Deliberately not copying

All items are **Oydin scope decisions/proposals**, not claims that every competitor has these features.

- **Finmir’s finance-department framing:** no NAS-21 selection, director workflow maze or balance-sheet literacy prerequisite; retain auditability behind everyday language ([Finmir](https://www.finmir.uz/en); custdev pains 7/10).
- **POS/ERP expansion:** no stock movements, cashier sales terminal, loyalty campaigns or assortment planning. Import useful totals; keep order economics bounded ([BILLZ](https://apps.apple.com/uz/app/billz-2-0-mobile/id6444280664); pain 4).
- **Banking as a feature checklist:** no payment execution, payroll signing, FX trading or lending by default ([Uzum Business](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=uz.kapitalbank.mobile24); pains 3/9).
- **Enterprise treasury:** no cash pooling, hedging, group-consolidation cockpit or FP&A converter ([Agicap](https://agicap.com/en-us/features/cashflow-forecast/); pain 10).
- **Indian regulatory/payment plumbing:** no UPI/GST clone; adopt approachable language and collection workflow, not unsupported rails ([OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/en/), [Vyapar](https://vyaparapp.in/guides/how-to-send-payment-reminders-in-vyapar)).
- **Pressure and fake precision:** no “3× faster” collection promise, automatic debt nagging, guaranteed 30-day accuracy or fixed red 20% alarm copied as universal truth ([OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/en/), [Finmir](https://www.finmir.uz/en)).
- **Engagement theatre:** no streaks, unsupported peer comparisons, weekly voice CFO performance or automatic local-season forecasts. This follows custdev §4 and the frozen contract, not a claim these competitors use them.
- **Feature-count competition:** no new integration backend merely because its logo appears elsewhere; catalogue status must match verified capability. No regime calculators or tax presets under the final tax decision.

## 5. Positioning against finmir.uz — five honest bullets

These are **proposed positioning directions**, not proven superiority or absence claims about Finmir.

1. **Same category, different first user.** Finmir explicitly serves owners **and finance managers** and foregrounds rigorous reports/NAS coding. Oydin should begin with an owner’s next cash decision, without requiring an accountant ([Finmir](https://www.finmir.uz/en); [Oydin product](../product.md)).
2. **Do not claim Telegram + UZS + forecasts as unique.** Finmir already advertises all three, plus UZ/RU/EN language alternatives in its public site. Differentiate on tested completion, clear confirmation and understandable next actions, not a false feature gap ([Finmir](https://www.finmir.uz/en), [Uzbek site](https://www.finmir.uz/)).
3. **From close today to decide tomorrow.** Lead Oydin with counted versus expected money and separate owner draws; Finmir’s public emphasis is reporting, forecasts and approvals. Its lack of cash-close/draw features is **unverified**; make a positive promise only after Oydin works ([Finmir](https://www.finmir.uz/en); [custdev recommendations](custdev.md#6-пять-конкретных-изменений-продукта--рекомендации-не-применённые-изменения)).
4. **Voice is optional convenience, honesty is the product.** Oydin can test RU/UZ voice-to-confirmed-draft with manual fallback, while explaining missing data and expected receipts. Finmir’s opened page verifies Telegram text entry, not whether it has voice; do not market “they have no voice” ([Finmir](https://www.finmir.uz/en); [custdev §4](custdev.md#4-что-оставить-в-проверке-а-что-не-имеет-подтверждённого-спроса)).
5. **Compete on time-to-trust, not an invented cheaper price.** Finmir offers a 14-day no-card trial; its paid price is unverified. Oydin’s 99–199k UZS range is an owner hypothesis/request-access proposition, not demonstrated willingness to pay or proof of price advantage ([Finmir](https://www.finmir.uz/en); [decisions](../decisions.md), [custdev](custdev.md)).
