# Custdev Oydin: cash-gap pain by segment (desk research)

Date: 18.11.2026. Method: desk research, **0 own interviews**. Result: a map of where public sources show cash-timing pain, what competitors charge, what Uzbek payroll/tax/payout rules imply for the forecast, and what to ask owners next. This file extends [custdev.md](custdev.md) and the mechanics in [../scenarios.md](../scenarios.md). It does not replace interviews. Sections 4a (pricing anchors) and 4b (payroll & tax rules) were merged from two further desk scouts; items under "Proposals pending product decision" in section 7 are not decided.

## 1. Method and caveat

- **Desk only.** Four scouts read public pages and search snippets: retail/merch/importers, services (café, beauty, training), projects (construction, events, couriers, farmers), competitors. Nobody was contacted, recruited or surveyed. No closed Telegram chats were read.
- **Source quality is uneven.** Many sources are vendor blogs, advertorials, or Russian/Kazakh material used as a regional proxy. Examples in accounting blogs often look illustrative, not real cases.
- **Evidence labels.** Confidence is high / medium / low per row, as set by the scouts. Items the scouts did not open are marked **[snippet]** (search result text only) and stay in the file with that mark. Nothing marked unverified was dropped.
- **No prevalence.** No source gives how many owners in a segment have a cash gap. Treat every row as "pain is described", not "pain is common".
- **Segments are unequal.** Merch printing has almost no direct evidence. Farmers and construction have the strongest news and official sources, but mostly about debts owed by the state or clusters, not about owner tooling.
- **Dates.** Some sources are old (Ferghana.ru 2014, Mondaq about 10 years, Kapital.uz 2021, NORMA.UZ 2018). They show the mechanism, not the 2026 level.

## 5. Objections

Sourced fears and counter-signals, not measured:

1. **Cost.** 25% of surveyed Kazakh sole proprietors avoid 1С «ссылаясь на ее дороговизну» (https://kapital.kz/business/97359/kazakhstanskiye-ip-prodolzhayut-vesti-biznes-na-tetradke-opros.html).
2. **Owners do not know some numbers.** Of 10 surveyed ИП, 9 did not know revenue per m² (same page).
3. **Trust in own judgement.** The jewelry owner hired help but concluded «никто не сделает лучше, чем ты сам» (https://kapital.uz/dengi-tesoro/). Prior research (IFC 2022): many women entrepreneurs rated their own financial management as satisfactory, so do not frame this as financial illiteracy (see [custdev.md](custdev.md)).
4. **Rent or contract lock-in.** Tenants feel «заложниками» of rent contracts; cutting costs may not be possible (https://kapital.uz/arenda-pay/).
5. **Timing outside the owner's control.** Bank and FX holds make a forecast wrong (https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/6852358, https://www.turanos.uz/news/valyutnyy-kontrol-v-uzbekistane-gayd-dlya-importerov).
6. **Retention is costly or unenforceable.** TheCRM: «удерживать людей юридически — плохая стратегия»; non-compete clauses weak in Uzbek labour law per TheCRM, lawyer check advised. Klerk: «постоянно повышать процент сильному мастеру» erodes salon economics (vendor content).
7. **Revenue-sharing with independent masters** carries bank and tax risk (115-FZ, Russia, ad content).
8. **Compliance uncertainty.** AliPos: owners wait for VAT 6% calculation rules before deciding (https://alipos.uz/ru/blog/issledovanie-obschepita-uzbekistana-2026-opros-vladelcev-12-voprosov).
9. **Reluctance to share financial data and distance from banks** (IFC 2022, see [custdev.md](custdev.md)).
10. **Different real problem.** Construction and farm cash gaps often come from a client, cluster or the state not paying, which is receivables, not bookkeeping (https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/07/21/klaster, https://labourmission.org/en/news-en/enter-engineering-the-company-that-received-multi-billion-dollar-projects-does-not-pay-its-workers/).
11. **Part of the schedule is run by someone else.** Farm settlements run through the Fund, banks and the exchange (https://agrofin.uz/ru/appeals/faq).

Scout hypotheses without a source (H), kept for the interviews: «касса у меня в голове и в Excel»; one-off event projects need no schedule; couriers count in their head; fuel price moves by itself so tracking does not help; large agencies use credit lines, small ones do not.

## 6. One 20-minute interview script

For owners who make the money decisions themselves, business at least 6 months, 0–15 employees. No pitch, past behaviour first, amounts may be hidden, record only with consent. Translate to UZ and have a native speaker check it before fieldwork. Merged and deduplicated from the four scouts' validation questions; ten at most.

1. What do you sell, and which money decisions do you make yourself? (2 min)
2. Walk me through your last order or purchase: when did the money come in and when did you spend it? (3 min)
3. Tell me about the last time money you expected did not arrive on time, or a payment was due and the balance did not cover it. What did you pay first, what did you postpone, what did it cost? If it never happened, how do you prevent it? (3 min)
4. Which payments fall in the next 14 days (rent, salary, supplier, fuel, seed, contractor), and where are they written down? Which move with sales, which are fixed by date? (2 min)
5. Who owes you money now, when did they promise to pay, and how do you track it? (2 min)
6. How did you decide how much you could take for yourself or spend last month? Show me the document if you can, without personal data. How did you calculate last month's profit and which costs might be missing? (3 min)
7. What do you use today (notebook, Excel, 1С, bank app, Telegram, booking tool)? How many hours a week does it take, what did you pay for the last tool, and why did you stop using a previous one? (2 min)
8. If a tool each morning said «today you can take X and pay Y; on Friday Z is short», what would you need it to show first, what would make you distrust it, and what would you do that day? (2 min)
9. What would you show a tool without handing over a bank statement (for example only balance and next incoming date), and what is off limits? (1 min)
10. Would you pay 100k or 200k UZS a month, or take a 14-day trial? What do you need to see before paying? Which money task do you need to solve this week, and will you try it on your depersonalised data and meet again? (Do not present a real charge.)

Segment probes for questions 2 and 3, only as follow-ups: production, advance/defects/labour; retail, purchase/stock; café, shift/write-offs; seller, commission/returns/payout; services, prepayment vs delivered service; training and salons, what happened to clients when a teacher or master left and how many were lost within six weeks; importers, prepayment and payment delays at the bank; farmers, who pays you and when (cluster, elevator, Fund); events, prepayments to venue and artists vs client payments. Do not assume these problems exist.

## 7. What this changes for Oydin

Only claims backed by the sources above.

1. **The tracked job is real in the competitor set.** The only UZ evidence on bank apps says they show balance and no forecast; one vendor study says users want a reminder before a limit is exceeded, not complex charts (Rocket Tech). No tool checked in the table frames the question as "how much can I spend or take today until payday". Caveat: Russian bank apps were not checked, Rocket Tech is a vendor study, and Uzum Business descriptions already list statements and deferred payments, so a single balance screen is not unique.
2. **Retail and importer evidence supports modelling fixed dates against seasonal revenue.** Rent and salary on a fixed schedule vs seasonal sales (kapital.uz 2021, medium confidence); supplier prepayment vs deferral terms and FX/bank holds (Kursiv snippet, Kommersant, Turanos snippet). The scenarios already treat supplier payments as obligations and buyer deferrals as orders with a balance and a date ([../scenarios.md](../scenarios.md)). Not yet modelled per that file: FX rate per payment, delivery time, buyer deferral limits.
3. **Farmers and construction: receivables dominate the evidence, so the "expected incoming" side matters as much as obligations.** Cluster debts and unpaid wages (Gazeta, Labour Mission) are about money not arriving. Oydin treats expected income as an order with a date and amount, not as a balance. A 90-day horizon limit and no harvest scenarios (scenarios.md) remain a gap for the segment with the strongest official sources (agrofin.uz rules: 80% in 3 working days, remainder by 31 December).
4. **Events: prepayment before non-refundable costs is the cited mechanism**, but only from an advertorial and Russian proxies. "Client cancelled" is not modelled yet (scenarios.md).
5. **Training centre: loss of a teacher's group with fixed rent is a documented fear**, but vendor-sourced and unsized. Group-based enrollment is not modelled (scenarios.md).
6. **Price band: no UZ evidence of demand.** Visible list prices run from 100,000 UZS/month (salon booking) to 1,490,000 UZS/month (training CRM); the 99–199k hypothesis is within the low end and still unvalidated.
7. **Merch printing remains the weakest-evidenced of the retail trio.** The pilot business «Print Lab Tashkent» uses demo data. Do not claim print-shop customers report prepayment pain; the one public signal is shops advertising "Без предоплаты".
8. **Do not use unverified items as facts:** the 15% prepayment and 90-day receivable rule, the 2,070 vs about 1,200 complaint counts, YCLIENTS 30% cancellations, TheCRM 3–5% churn, 2014 farm figures, insurance-backed import finance rates.

### Proposals pending product decision (from sections 4a and 4b)

Not decided and not implemented. Each is a candidate change to the forecast or pricing; Main/product owner decides.

1. **Weekend/holiday payday shift (proposal).** Move a salary payday that falls on a weekend or public holiday to the preceding working day (Labour Code Art. 253 per tax-legal.uz). Today's Friday question is the natural case: a Saturday payday is due on Friday. Holiday calendar source still needed.
2. **Two paydays per month, ≤16 days apart (proposal).** Model payroll as two instalments, not one monthly lump (Art. 253).
3. **Social tax reserve (proposal).** Add an outflow of 12% of the prior month's payroll expense due by the 15th of the next month (leaderaudit.uz). Show it as a separate obligation, not inside salary.
4. **NDFL (proposal: do not add a date).** Keep it inside the salary outflow; no remittance deadline was verified. Mark [INFERENCE] in any UI copy.
5. **Marketplace payout lag (proposal).** For Uzum sellers, treat goods sold in the last 10 days as not yet cash and place incoming cash on the payout schedule (biweekly 7th/21st default; weekend payouts move to the next working day; up to 4 working days late possible). Source: seller.uzum.uz.
6. **Card acquiring lag (proposal).** Use a 1–3 business-day lag [INFERENCE]; no Uzbek settlement timing was found.
7. **Rent as USD-pegged fixed outflow (proposal).** Convert at the CB rate; timing unverified, so let the owner enter the date.
8. **Late-pay cost warning (proposal).** Art. 333 compensation (10% of the CB refinancing rate per day) and Art. 253 ban on postponing for cash shortage make a missed Friday payroll a legal cost, which supports the product's framing; wording needs legal review.
9. **Pricing (proposal, own inference, not sourced prices).** Anchor below core POS/ERP tools: about 49k–99k UZS/mo individual, 150k–250k SMB with payroll and several accounts; free one-forecast tier; monthly and annual billing; test a bank-partner add-on since Kapitalbank SME bundles cost 100k–250k/mo. Section 4a sets the evidence; this still has no revealed willingness to pay.
