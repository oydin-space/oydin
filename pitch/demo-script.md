# Oydin — three-minute live demo

**Run in English; speaker cues are in Russian.** Use a prepared demo owner account and English UI. The clock is for the walkthrough, not for waiting on a network response. Keep the demo data labelled as demo data; do not quote a synthetic balance as customer traction. If a screen has no gap, second business, or generated calculator, say what is actually on screen rather than pretending it does.

| Time | Exact action on screen | Say (one sentence) | Заметка выступающему |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:13 | Open `https://oydin.sayfi.uz/login`, switch language to **English** if needed, then click **View demo**; wait for `/app` (or finish onboarding if the account is not seeded). | “Let’s look at a small business from the owner’s side.” | Сначала дождись Home; если открывается onboarding, не делай вид, что данные уже есть. |
| 0:13–0:28 | On **Home** (`/app`), point to the pulse ring and **Safe to spend today** amount. | “This is the pulse, and this number is what the business can safely spend today.” | Не читай синтетическую сумму как реальный результат бизнеса. |
| 0:28–0:42 | Point to the warning directly below the pulse and the cash chart; if it says **Money runs short on …**, show the date and reason; otherwise point to the **no gap** status. | “Here’s the next cash gap and why it happens—or, if there isn’t one, the app says so.” | Причина берётся с экрана, не придумывай дату или сумму. |
| 0:42–0:57 | Click **Operations** (`/app/operations`) → **Add entry**; show amount, account and category fields, then close the form without saving a made-up entry. | “The owner can record money coming in or going out without opening accounting software.” | Покажи реальную форму, не добавляй случайную сумму в демо. |
| 0:57–1:13 | Click **Home** → the microphone in the quick-entry card; say a real prepared demo operation, stop recording, and show the draft confirmation sheet; if microphone access is unavailable, click **Add manually** instead. | “Voice can draft an entry too, but a person checks it before it’s saved.” | Заранее проверь микрофон и согласие на AI; если черновика нет, используй ручной ввод, не утверждай, что он создан. |
| 1:13–1:43 | Click **Chat** (`/app/chat`), type **Will we have enough for payroll on the 10th?**, click **Send**, and show the answer; if it contains a calculator, click **Save as tool**. | “I can ask about payroll directly, and when AI builds a calculator, I can save it for later.” | Вопрос точный; ответ и калькулятор не гарантированы, показывай только то, что вернулось. |
| 1:43–1:59 | Click **Tools** in the desktop sidebar (or **More** → **Tools** on mobile), then under **Templates** click **Open** on an available template. | “There are reusable calculator templates here even if we don’t wait for a new one to generate.” | Если шаблонов нет, покажи состояние экрана, не называй несуществующий шаблон. |
| 1:59–2:14 | Click **Plan** (`/app/plan`), open **Can I afford it**; point to the UZS/USD toggle, amount and date fields, and **Check** (run it only with a prepared demo amount). | “Can I afford it checks a proposed purchase against the cash plan.” | Не подставляй произвольную сумму; при отсутствии подготовленной суммы покажи форму. |
| 2:14–2:29 | In **Plan**, click **Goals**, then **Taxes**; point to **Configure** on the tax reserve if it appears. | “Goals sit next to an optional tax reserve that the owner sets, not an automatic tax bill.” | Резерв — планирование, не расчёт налога; настройку сохранять не нужно. |
| 2:29–2:40 | Click **More** → **Settings** → **Notifications** (desktop: sidebar **Settings** → **Notifications**); show **Telegram bot** and **Morning brief**; if a linked bot chat is prepared, open it and send `/brief`. | “The linked Telegram bot can send a morning brief and alerts, so the owner doesn’t need to keep this page open.” | Не обещай фиксированное время: час выбирает пользователь; если бот не подключён, показывай настройки, не имитируй сообщение. |
| 2:40–2:51 | Click **More** → **Team** (desktop: **Team**), show role choices under **Invite** without sending an invitation; click the business name in the header or sidebar to open the business switcher. | “The owner can assign owner, accountant or seller roles and switch between businesses they belong to.” | Если второго бизнеса нет, только покажи switcher; приглашение не отправляй. |
| 2:51–3:00 | Click **More** → **Settings** → **For accountant** (desktop: **Settings** → **For accountant**), then **New link**; show the period and expiry fields without creating a link for a real person. | “And an accountant can get a time-limited, read-only report link instead of full access.” | PDF доступен для созданной ссылки; не создавай публичную ссылку ради кадра. |

**If AI is slow:** “That answer is still loading; I’ll show the calculator templates rather than wait on it.” If the answer arrives without a calculator: “This answer didn’t generate one, so I won’t claim it did; the Tools templates are already available.”

**If voice is slow or blocked:** “The microphone is still processing; the same entry can be added manually, and nothing is saved without confirmation.”

**Founder confirmation before presenting:** confirm the demo account has seeded entries and a visible gap; confirm the prepared voice phrase and microphone/AI consent; confirm whether the payroll answer actually yields a calculator; confirm which templates load; confirm a linked Telegram bot is available for `/brief`; confirm whether this owner belongs to a second business; confirm any demo amounts or dates you plan to show aloud. Do not present Payme, Click, Uzum Market, Moysklad, Billz, Didox, Faktura, bank presets, Google Sheets or the voice CFO call as working; these are coming soon or environment-dependent, not part of this walkthrough.
