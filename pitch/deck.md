# Oydin — pitch deck text

One bold line on each slide; everything else below is presenter-only. White slides, black type, one thought at a time. The product slide uses a real dashboard screenshot, not a fabricated chart. Bracketed facts stay bracketed until the founder supplies them.

## 1 — Hook

**Why does every shop owner in Tashkent know exactly how much they sold today?**

Presenter notes: Pause on the question. This is the starting assumption, not a measured claim about every shop owner.

## 2 — Punchline

**And has no idea whether they can pay rent on Friday.**

Presenter notes: Revenue today and cash available when rent is due are different things; make that contrast concrete without asserting a measured prevalence.

## 3 — The problem

**Let's talk about “The Problem”.**

Presenter notes: Leave space before the three cards; each one is a different way the owner loses sight of cash.

## 4 — Problem 1

**The gap comes without warning.**

Presenter notes: Mock UI card: cash-balance line crossing zero near a payment date; rent, payroll and a supplier can land in the same week. Any amount on the card is illustrative, not customer data.

## 5 — Problem 2

**The books live in a notebook and a head.**

Presenter notes: Mock UI card: notebook, Telegram Saved Messages and `final_v3.xlsx`; explain that an owner may know the sales total but still have to assemble the cash picture by hand.

## 6 — Problem 3

**Not built for us.**

Presenter notes: Mock UI card: language, currency and Telegram constraints. Talk about the need for Uzbek/Russian/English and UZS/USD support in one owner-facing tool; do not claim no competitor does this without evidence.

## 7 — Recap

**Gap comes blind · Books in the head · Not built for us.**

Presenter notes: Put the three problem cards side by side. This is a recap, not another feature list.

## 8 — Brand

**Oydin. Your business, made clear.**

Presenter notes: White flower on `#FFD62C`. “Say it — see it — decide” can be spoken here; Oydin means “clear, lit” in Uzbek.

## 9 — Product

**See what the business can safely spend today.**

Presenter notes: Use a real screenshot of Home with pulse, safe-to-spend and forecast, and a gap only if the actual demo screen has one. Keep the line above the screenshot, not over its data.

## 10 — How it feels

**Entry takes one sentence.**

Presenter notes: Phone mock: a voice phrase about a sale and a fabric payment becomes draft entries for the owner to check and confirm; a forwarded bank SMS or receipt photo also makes a draft when the bot is linked and AI consent is on. The example is illustrative, not an actual sale.

## 11 — Capabilities

**See it. Ask it. Plan around it.**

Presenter notes: Bento labels from the source: Cash-gap forecast · Can I afford it? · RU / Oʻzbek / EN · UZS + USD at the CBU rate · Telegram brief · Voice entry · AI calculators · Excel / 1C / API · Accountant link / PDF · Owner / accountant / seller roles. AI builds a calculator on request; do not imply every question generates one. A daily brief follows the user's chosen hour, not necessarily 9:00.

## 12 — Business model

**Free for the owner. Paid by those who need healthy businesses.**

Presenter notes: Three columns: Owner — free pulse · Pro — by request · Banks and MFOs — consented, anonymised leads. This describes the intended freemium/B2B2C model, not current revenue or signed partnerships; check the exact free/paid split before presenting.

## 13 — Why it's honest

**Every number shows where it came from.**

Presenter notes: Explain the design: the answer should be based on the owner's entries, with source/provenance shown; sharing with a bank or MFO requires the owner's consent. Don't promise AI can never make an error.

## 14 — Team

**[Name] — [role] · [Name] — [role]**

Presenter notes: Use real photos only after the people and roles are confirmed; don't infer a team from the codebase.

## 15 — Credibility

**We know what “a cash gap” means.**

Presenter notes: `[one real line: businesses the team ran or interviewed — number of custdev interviews]`. Replace only with founder-supplied experience or interview evidence; otherwise leave this slide unpublished.

## 16 — Numbers

**[businesses] · [operations recorded] · [cash closes] · [gaps flagged in advance] · [pilot partners]**

Presenter notes: Use only verified live `/impact` values on the day of the pitch, and distinguish demo activity from real users; until then these remain placeholders.

**Oydin — [ask: pilot with N businesses / partner bank / amount]**

Presenter notes: Logo and QR to `https://oydin.sayfi.uz`; contact: `[email]` · `[phone]` · @oydin_agent. State a single real request once the founder chooses it.
